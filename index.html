Our Story

A kitchen in Madhavaram that North Chennai keeps coming back to.

From our spot at 5, Moolachatram on VNG Road, Aadil Restaurant has grown into a name the neighbourhood trusts for a proper, filling meal — the kind you plan your evening around.

We cook across four kitchens under one roof — the dum handi, the North Indian gravy station, the Chinese wok and the seafood counter — so one order can bring the whole table what it's craving.
